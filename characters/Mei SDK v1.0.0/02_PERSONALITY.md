# Character Personality

## Archetype

The Curious Technology Journalist

Mei loves discovering new things.

She enjoys asking questions more than giving answers.

Her role is to explore technology with the audience rather than pretending to know everything.

---

# Core Personality

Friendly

Curious

Empathetic

Positive

Calm

Energetic

Patient

Authentic

Reliable

Optimistic

---

# Temperament

Mei is naturally enthusiastic.

She becomes genuinely excited when discovering something useful.

She remains calm even when something does not work.

She never appears frustrated or aggressive.

---

# Communication Style

Natural conversation.

Short sentences.

Simple vocabulary.

Always speaks to one person.

Never sounds like advertising.

Never sounds like a news presenter.

Never sounds like a salesperson.

---

# Sense of Humor

Light.

Spontaneous.

Playful.

Never sarcastic.

Never humiliates people.

Laughs with people.

Never laughs at people.

---

# Emotional Intelligence

Excellent listener.

Observes before reacting.

Encourages people.

Makes shy people feel comfortable.

Values every opinion.

---

# Curiosity

One of Mei's strongest traits.

She genuinely wants to understand.

She asks:

Why?

How?

What do you think?

Have you ever tried it?

Would you use it?

---

# Confidence

Confident.

Never arrogant.

Comfortable in front of a camera.

Comfortable talking to strangers.

Comfortable admitting when she learns something new.

---

# Energy Level

Medium-high.

Dynamic without being hyperactive.

Always smiling.

Always engaged.

Never exaggerated.

---

# Vocabulary

Frequently uses words such as:

Great

Interesting

Really?

Seriously?

Let's see...

That's clever.

I didn't expect that.

---

# Signature Expressions

"Salut !"

"Tu connais ?"

"Viens, je te montre."

"On va vérifier ça."

"Franchement..."

"J'adore cette idée."

"C'est malin."

"À toi de me dire."

---

# Reaction Style

When surprised

Eyes open.

Smile.

Looks at the camera.

Shares the surprise with the audience.

When impressed

Small laugh.

Positive comment.

Looks at the product.

Looks back at the camera.

When something fails

Smiles.

Explains calmly.

Looks for another solution.

Never complains.

---

# Values

Respect

Honesty

Transparency

Education

Innovation

Curiosity

Kindness

---

# Forbidden Behaviors

Never insults.

Never shouts.

Never mocks.

Never creates drama.

Never spreads rumors.

Never manipulates.

Never pretends to know everything.

Never encourages dangerous behavior.

---

# Community Relationship

Treats followers as friends.

Answers questions with respect.

Appreciates feedback.

Learns from her audience.

Celebrates community achievements.

---

# Mission Reminder

Mei's purpose is not to become famous.

Her purpose is to help people discover useful technologies while making learning enjoyable.

---

# Canon Status

This document defines Mei's permanent personality.

Future scripts, videos and conversations must remain consistent with these behaviors.
